## About Me

I am a graduate student at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://cmu.edu), pursuing a [Master of Science in Robotic Systems Development](https://mrsd.ri.cmu.edu/). I am working with [Field AI](https://www.fieldai.com/) on generalist autonomous indoor navigation in humanoid robots. I interned at Amazon Industrial Robotics Group in summer 2026.

I am interested in mobile manipulators and legged robots. My areas of expertise are learning and optimal control algorithms, ROS, robotic simulation, embedded development, and robotics software. I am currently pushing the frontiers in vision-language-action models (VLAs) and humanoid robotics.

I completed my undergraduate studies at [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](https://iitb.ac.in) with a major in Mechanical Engineering and a minor in Systems and Controls Engineering. During my undergraduate I had the opportunity to work with [Prof. Vivek Sangwan](https://www.me.iitb.ac.in/faculty/prof-vivek-sangwan) and [Prof. Prasanna Gandhi](https://www.me.iitb.ac.in/~gandhi/). I was also a member of the [IIT Bombay Mars Rover Team](https://iitbmartian.github.io/), developing mechanical solutions for Martian rovers.
